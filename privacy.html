Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

ClipsFactory does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All processing happens locally on your device.

2. TikTok Authentication

When you authenticate with TikTok via OAuth 2.0, your access token is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com).

3. Video Content

Videos are processed entirely on your local machine and uploaded directly to TikTok via their official Content Posting API. No video data passes through any third-party server.

4. No Analytics

ClipsFactory does not use any analytics, tracking, or telemetry services.

5. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact the developer via GitHub.
